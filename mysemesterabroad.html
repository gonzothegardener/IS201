For my from-scratch page I am going to demonstrate my newly acquired web development skills by ranking some of the most memorable experiences I’ve had while studying abroad this semester.
  • Lyon, France - Most Memorable Experiences
    1. Visiting the Musée des Beaux-Arts and finding my favorite painting of all time, Le Joueur de Flûte by Jacques Martin
    2. Walking by the river on our way to la basilique Notre-Dame de Fouvrière, and hearing a man play Vienna by Billy Joel on the saxophone
    3. Having the best food ever, including a waffle covered in chocolate and fresh fruit, and incredible ravioli at Café 203
  • Geneva, Switzerland - Most Memorable Experiences
    1. The food!! I had the best steak I’ve ever tasted (it was so expensive but so worth it), swiss chocolate, crème brûlée, and of course fondue with bread and potatoes
    2. The entire city was so beautiful and clean. The river was so blue and had swans in it. We took a walk through a park by the museum and watched the sun set.
    3. Our hotel was so cute! It had 4 little books that I remember reading with my friend while we dressed up and got ready to go out for dinner.
  • Paris, France - Most Memorable Experiences
    1. I really loved going to the Musée d’Orsay. The impressionist collection was beautiful, and afterwards we got crêpes and ate them by the Seine.
    2. I loved going to Disneyland Paris! We ate inside the Pirates of the Caribbean ride and ironically saw a dead rat on our way to the park the first day (rip Ratatouille).
    3. Walking through Luxembourg gardens and the streets of Paris for hours while catching up with my best friend was one of my favorite things.
  • London, England - Most Memorable Experiences
    1. As soon as we arrived in London we bought tickets an went to go see Hamilton on the West End. It was so good!
    2. The food is always memorable. Highlights included the burgers we had before going to see Hamilton, our hot pot lunch by the British Museum, pastries from the bakery by our hotel, and nachos and cheese bread from a pub.
    3. Hearing people speak with British accents in real life was so entertaining.
  • Edinburgh, Scotland - Most Memorable Experiences
    1. We signed up for a workshop to make our own Islander bags. It took us forever to decide what we wanted, but they both turned out really cute!
    2. After hiking Calton Hill we were exhausted and stumbled upon the best restaraunt ever called The Ivy. For dessert we got ice cream that was surrounded by a chocolcate shell which metled away when warm choclate was poured over it.
    3. Going shopping on royal mile was so fun, all the shops were so cute!
